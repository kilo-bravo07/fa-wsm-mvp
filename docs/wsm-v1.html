Methodology, assumptions and known gaps

Formula

S1 = M1·A + M2·B + M3·C + M4·D → S2 = S1·E if recent → RS = S2·F if the price condition holds. Higher RS ranks earlier.

Metric sources

M1 = PLP clicks ÷ PLP views · M2 = PLP add-to-cart ÷ PLP clicks · M3 = SKU orders ÷ all flower orders in the window · M4 = PDP add-to-cart ÷ PDP views · M5 = days since last order · M6 = price against the median of its bouquet-type peer group.

Normalisation

M1–M4 are rates on different scales — page CTR runs near 1.6%, ATC rate near 18%, order share near 0.09%. Summing them raw would let one metric dominate through its units alone, so each is mapped to 0–1 across the page before weighting. Min–max is the default; percentile rank is more robust when one outlier compresses everyone else.

Smoothing

A product with 5 clicks and 2 add-to-carts shows a 40% ATC rate. Unsmoothed it outranks genuinely strong products on noise. Each ratio is pulled toward the page's pooled rate in proportion to how little traffic supports it. Set Smoothing k to Off to see what the unguarded model would do.

Cold start

50 of the 240 products were created after the analysis window opened and have no fair history. Scoring them at zero would bury them permanently — a self-fulfilling prophecy — so they take the median score of established products until they accumulate data.

M5 barely discriminates

At the specified three-month threshold, 237 of 240 products qualify as recent. Multiplying 98.7% of the catalogue by 1.4 changes no relative ordering — it is arithmetically the same as having no M5 at all. Recency was designed for a cake catalogue where long-tail SKUs go dormant; a live flower page sells nearly everything nearly every day. Shorten the window above to make the modifier do work.

M4 overlaps M2

The two add-to-cart metrics correlate at ρ = 0.77 — they measure much the same propensity at two surfaces, so the effective weighting is more concentrated than 45/25/20/10 suggests. Also note only about 10% of PDP traffic arrives from this page, so M4 largely reflects site-wide product strength rather than this page's.

Peer groups for M6

The catalogue's Category field is "Main" for every row, so bouquet type stands in. Groups below 10 products fall back to the page-wide benchmark — with a 3-product group every member sits at its own median and the modifier stops discriminating. The benchmark is the median, not the mean: one ₹45,045 product drags the Bunches mean to ₹2,395 against a median of ₹995, which would mark 70% of the group "below average".

Joins

PLP metrics join on GUID — verified 240/240 with matching names. PDP metrics and the current sequence carry no ID column and were matched on product name; both matched 240/240, but production must join on GUID. Two products here differ only by an apostrophe.

Open items

The GA4 date window was not stated on the extracts and is assumed to align with the order window shown at the top. Position bias is not corrected: current position correlates with CTR at ρ = 0.07 (not significant), so measured rates are not meaningfully contaminated by placement — unusual, and it means raw CTR is more trustworthy here than on a tuned page.
