Listing Page Sequencing — /all-flowers

Products are scored on four signals, then dealt into a repeating thirty-slot template that holds five price bands plus combos and hampers. Boosted SKUs are inserted on top, outside the thirty. Every control is live.

Step 1 · Score

Four signals, each normalised 0–1 across the catalogue, then weighted. Rates are smoothed toward the site average so thin-traffic products are neither flattered nor buried.

Step 2 · The thirty-slot template

Each position asks for a bucket; the bucket hands over its highest-scoring unplaced product. The template repeats for the whole page. Edit any position.

Bucket supply

How many products each bucket holds, and how many complete sets of thirty it can fill before the slot starts falling back to a neighbouring band.

Boost insertions

Up to four per set of thirty. An insertion adds a product rather than replacing one, so a set carrying two insertions shows thirty-two — the base order never shifts. A SKU already placed, or out of stock, is skipped.

The page

Every product in final position. A faded title with a dot means the slot's own bucket was empty and the fallback chain filled it.

Methodology, assumptions and known gaps

Scoring

CTR = clicks ÷ impressions · ATC rate = PDP add-to-cart ÷ clicks · Conversion = orders ÷ clicks · Orders = order count. Each is normalised 0–1 across the catalogue before weighting, because they are rates on very different scales and summing them raw would let one dominate through its units alone.

The two "÷ clicks" denominators

This is what the PRD specifies and what the page loads with, but the data does not support it. Add-to-carts and orders arrive through routes that never touched the listing, while clicks are counted only on it — so the ratio is between two different populations. Measured here: 213 of 346 products exceed 100% on ATC rate, the median is 132% and the maximum 1,000%. Switched to PDP views, nothing exceeds 100% and the median falls to 9.2%. The switch changes 14 of the top 30, so it is not cosmetic.

Combo orders are not being captured

Only 3 of 60 combos report any purchase in the PDP extract, yet 35 of them sold in the earlier order file — Mystic Orchids N Cake Combo at 156 orders and Red Rose N Truffle at 120 both show zero here. Two of the four signals are order-derived, so 40% of the combo score is currently reading zero for almost the whole bucket, and the Combo slots are being decided on CTR and ATC alone. Make Your Own Combo takes the top combo slot on 315 purchases against 1 add-to-cart, which cannot be right either.

Three pools, three different pages

Flower metrics come from /all-flowers, combos from /combos/flowers-and-cakes, hampers from /hampers/same-day-delivery. A product's CTR therefore reflects the page it was measured on. Buckets contain this — combos only ever compete with combos — but the fallback chain crosses buckets, so a combo filling a price slot is being compared on a rate earned elsewhere.

Fallback

The PRD says an empty pool borrows the equivalent SKU from a later batch. That cannot terminate once a pool is globally exhausted, and pulling forward leaves a hole that cascades. Instead a price slot falls through to the nearest band by price, then to the best remaining product of any bucket; combo and hamper slots fall straight through to best remaining.

Impression floor

Below the floor a product is not scored on its own rates — it takes the median score of its own bucket, so a thin combo is judged against combos rather than against the whole catalogue. Without it, smoothing hands a no-data product the page-average rate and it outranks products with real measured performance.

Missing data

No stock status in any extract, so every product is treated as in stock and the two stock rules are implemented but unverified. No price for combos or hampers — not fatal, since both go to their own buckets regardless, but it means combo-versus-price-band precedence cannot be checked. No launch dates for combos or hampers.

Window

Flower orders are sliced to the 30 days ending 22 Sep 2026. The GA4 extracts for all three pools arrived without a stated range, and combo and hamper orders come from PDP Purchase whose window is also unstated. Re-pull everything on one declared window before quoting these figures.
