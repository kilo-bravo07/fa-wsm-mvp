Products are scored on four behavioural signals, then dealt into a repeating ten-slot template that holds a price mix, a new-arrival seat and a featured seat. Every control below is live.

Step 1 · Score

Four signals, each normalised 0–1 across the page, then weighted. Rates are smoothed toward the site average so thin-traffic products aren't flattered or punished by noise.

Step 2 · Slots

Each position in a batch of ten asks for a slot type, and the type takes the highest-scoring unplaced product from its pool. Edit any position below.

Methodology, assumptions and known gaps

Scoring

CTR = PLP clicks ÷ PLP impressions · PDP ATC rate = PDP add-to-cart ÷ PDP views · Conversion = orders ÷ PLP clicks · Orders = order count. Each is normalised 0–1 across the page before weighting, because they are rates on very different scales — page CTR runs near 1.6%, PDP ATC near 14% — and summing them raw would let one dominate through its units alone.

Smoothing

A product with 5 clicks and 2 add-to-carts posts a 40% rate. Unsmoothed it outranks genuine performers on noise. Each ratio is shrunk toward the page's pooled rate in proportion to how little traffic supports it. Set smoothing to Off to see the unguarded version.

The fallback chain

The PRD's strict ₹750–1200 M band qualifies 56 products, but the template asks for 166 M seats across 24 batches, and 58 products priced below ₹750 belong to no band the PRD names. Each slot therefore falls through an ordered chain when its own pool empties (M → LOW → H, H → M → LOW, N and F → M → LOW → H). The strict band governs the top of the page, where the price mix matters, and every product still lands exactly once. Editable in config/config.json.

Three signals share a funnel

PDP add-to-cart rate, conversion rate and order count all measure stages of the same journey, so 65% of the weight sits downstream of the click. They will move together. That isn't wrong, but the effective weighting is more concentrated than 35/25/20/20 reads.

Stock status is missing

No extract carries it, so every product is treated as in stock. Three requirements depend on it — out-of-stock barred from the top 10, pushed to the end of the page, and the guardrail metric. The logic is implemented and will activate as soon as a GUID, in_stock column arrives; until then it is untested against real data.

Window

The PRD specifies a trailing 30 days. Sales data ends 22 Sep 2026, so the window is the last 30 days of available data rather than of the calendar. The GA4 extracts arrived without a stated range and are assumed to match — they are currently a longer aggregate, which inflates CTR and PDP rates relative to a true 30-day pull. Re-pull both at 30 days before quoting these numbers.

New arrivals run out

The N pool holds whatever launched inside the window — currently a dozen products, nearly all one Korean range dropped on a single day. The template wants one per batch across 24 batches. Use the manual list above to seed it, or accept that N stops appearing partway down and the slot falls back to M.
